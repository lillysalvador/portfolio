Reconnecting with Everyday Consumers

A brand strategy project addressing Under Armour’s declining relevance with younger consumers. Through market research, competitor analysis, and audience insights, we identified an opportunity to build on the brand’s athletic roots while expanding into everyday lifestyle wear. Our “Grow Into More” strategy focuses on evolving Under Armour from a brand chosen for performance into one consumers choose for both style and self-expression, creating new ways for the brand to connect with people beyond sports.

Building a New Current in Jacksonville

A semester-long project creating a brand identity for a fictional NBA expansion team in my hometown of Jacksonville, Florida. Designed the team’s visual identity and applied it to marketing materials, including postcards, booklets, and brochures, using Adobe Illustrator and InDesign. Brought the designs to life through hands-on print production using the HP Indigo 7900 and inline CMYK presses.
